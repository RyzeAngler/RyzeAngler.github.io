I integrated knowledge bases, session-file RAG, short- and long-term memory,
local tools, MCP, and Skill into reusable agent templates. I designed a ReAct
execution engine and unified tool protocol, including BM25 Chinese retrieval,
lightweight LLM routing, fallback, circuit breaking, clarification, and human
handoff.
